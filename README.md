Paperless Mission - An EdTech and Telecom AI Research Initiative
By Er. Fareheen, Telecom AI Research Scientist
An Initiative for Jawadul Manzoor Foundation (Proposed)

Introduction:
I am a Telecom AI Research Scientist. Jawadul Manzoor Educational Initiative is my personal educational mission. My dream is to register it as a Foundation in future to provide free education and health facilities. Currently it is a proposed initiative and research project.

My Research is based on one unified pedagogical rule: Solve, Assess, Research, Try Again.

My Research Projects:

Project 1: Telecom AI and Telecom Tools - Main Project
This is my core research domain. I have developed automated tools for Telecom Engineers. Instead of manual formula calculation, the engineer directly inputs field values like dBm to Watt, Fiber Loss, Link Budget. The system uses logic to instantly compute and generate accurate report. This saves 90 percent time and removes human error.

Project 2: Cambridge Maths - Foundation Building Module
To build strong engineers, strong fundamentals in Maths from grassroots level are required. I have digitized Cambridge Maths curriculum into interactive worksheets with 40 diverse questions per topic.

Project 3: Cambridge English - Foundation Building Module
Similarly, Cambridge English Learner's Book and Workbook is digitized for conceptual clarity and grammar mastery.

Project 4: Cambridge Science - Upcoming Research Module
In future, Cambridge Science will also be added under same rule.

Unified Research Methodology - The Core Rule:
All my projects work on one single rule.
Step 1 Solve: User solves the worksheet or uses the tool.
Step 2 Assess: System instantly evaluates and gives score, for example 20 out of 40.
Step 3 Research: User researches his mistakes.
Step 4 Try Again: User clicks Reset and Try Again till mastery, 40 out of 40.
This loop continues till mastery is achieved. Complete mastery without paper use.

Impact - EPI Report:
Our paperless model saves 8000 plus A4 sheets annually. Aligned with UN SDG 4 and SDG 12.
